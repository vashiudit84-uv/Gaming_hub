Why this hooks you

  • Sub-2-second loop. Every round resolves almost instantly, so you're never more than a heartbeat from the next attempt.
  • Variable reward. Bonus Pulses fire at random, unpredictable moments — the same intermittent-reward schedule that makes slot machines hard to walk away from.
  • Rising difficulty. The dot speeds up and the zone tightens as you improve, keeping you right at the edge of your skill (flow state) instead of bored or overwhelmed.
  • Streak & loss aversion. One miss wipes your multiplier. The fear of losing progress pulls harder than the promise of gaining it.
  • Zero-friction restart. Game over to game-on in a single tap — no loading, no menu, nothing between you and "one more try."
  • A moving personal best. Your own record becomes a goal that keeps re-generating itself.

Same core psychology the industry uses — minus the parts that cross into manipulation, like paid loot boxes, guilt-trip push notifications, or pay-to-skip walls.
